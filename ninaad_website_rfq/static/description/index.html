Overview

Website Product Enquiry

The Website Product Enquiry module for Odoo replaces direct purchasing on the website with an enquiry workflow. Visitors add products to an enquiry list, choose the quantities and send the request, and the module turns it into a lead / opportunity in Odoo CRM.

Logged-in users have the lead created for their own contact. Public users fill in a short form, and the module reuses an existing contact when the email or phone number matches, or creates a new contact otherwise. The requested products and quantities appear in a dedicated Products tab on the CRM record.

Features

  • Adds an Add to Enquiry button on website product pages for both logged-in and public users.
  • Replaces direct website purchasing with an enquiry list that shows the selected products with quantities.
  • Logged-in users: creates a CRM lead / opportunity for their own contact with the selected products and quantities.
  • Public users: collects name, email, phone number, company and location through an enquiry form.
  • Automatically matches an existing contact by email or phone number; creates a new contact only when none is found.
  • Adds a Products tab on the CRM lead / opportunity showing the requested products and quantities.
  • Reduces manual data entry and keeps the sales pipeline updated with website enquiries.

Configuration

STEP 1 — ADD PRODUCTS TO THE ENQUIRY LIST

Open the website and go to any product page.

  • The Add to Enquiry button is shown on the product, for both logged-in and public users.
  • Click it to add the product to the enquiry list.
STEP 2 — ENQUIRY LIST

Click the enquiry list (cart) icon on the website.

  • Displays every selected product together with its quantity.
  • Adjust the quantities, then continue to send the enquiry.
STEP 3 — LOGGED-IN USER

When a logged-in user submits the enquiry:

  • A lead / opportunity is created automatically for the user's own contact.
  • Open CRM and select the record. The selected products and quantities are listed under the new Products tab.
STEP 4 — PUBLIC USER ENQUIRY FORM

When a public (not logged-in) user submits the enquiry, a form opens with these fields:

  • Name, Email, Phone Number, Company and Location.
  • After the form is submitted, the module searches for a contact with the same email or phone number.
  • If a matching contact is found, the lead / opportunity is created for that contact. If not, a new contact is created and the lead / opportunity is created for it.
STEP 5 — CRM LEAD / OPPORTUNITY

Go to CRM and open the lead / opportunity created from the website.

  • The Products tab lists the requested products and quantities.
  • The sales team can follow up with the customer from the record.
